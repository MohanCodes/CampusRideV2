01 / Why CampusRide

Getting off campus
should feel simple.

A grocery run, a flight home, a day in LA. Students without cars often face expensive rides or inconvenient transit. Meanwhile, other students are making the same trips with empty seats.

CampusRide brings those students together through a dedicated carpooling app.

04 / A few things to know

Before the
first ride.

Is CampusRide available yet?

The mobile app is currently in development. This website introduces the project and our planned experience. App availability and launch details will be shared as they are confirmed.

Who is CampusRide for?

College students who need a ride beyond campus, and student drivers who already have a trip planned and are open to sharing empty seats. We’re starting with the Claremont Colleges.

How is this different from ride-hailing?

CampusRide is built around trips that student drivers already plan to make. Riders connect with someone going in a similar direction rather than requesting an on-demand professional driver.

How much will a ride cost?

The goal is to make shared trips affordable by splitting travel costs. Final pricing and payment details are still being developed and will be communicated before launch.

What about safety?

Safety is a priority as we develop the app. Eligibility requirements and safety features will be explained before launch, so students can understand how the service works before using it.
